# Lab 4 — Build a five-slide story before designing slides

## Scenario

You must brief a college leadership team on the workshop registration data.

## Build this story arc

1. Opening: the decision leadership must make.
2. Evidence: the two numbers that matter most.
3. Meaning: what those numbers imply for delivery.
4. Action: the recommended setup.
5. Close: the approval or next step required.

Use `story-arc-template.md`. Each slide title must state a takeaway, not a topic. Do not begin slide design until the five titles form a coherent argument.
