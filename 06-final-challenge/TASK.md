# Final challenge — Brief, build, verify, present

Choose one scenario:

1. College workshop registration helper
2. Community event information page
3. Small-business enquiry tracker

## Required evidence

- `brief.md`: context, outcome, inputs, constraints, acceptance criteria (start from [brief-template.md](brief-template.md))
- A visible artifact: document, analysis, slide story, or small code change
- A verification result: source table, calculation, test output, or review checklist
- `reflection.md`: assumptions, limitations, and one next improvement (start from [reflection-template.md](reflection-template.md))

## Demo format — 90 seconds

1. Problem and audience — 20 seconds
2. What the AI helped produce — 30 seconds
3. How you verified it — 25 seconds
4. What remains uncertain — 15 seconds
